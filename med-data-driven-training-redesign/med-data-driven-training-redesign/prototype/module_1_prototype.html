A quick fact before we start

How you open the first two minutes shapes what a patient is willing to tell you for the rest of the visit — more than almost anything else you'll do in the interview.

Let's look at three techniques that make a strong opening.

🗣️
Open-Ended Opening
Tap to reveal
Start broad and let the patient set the direction — e.g., "What brings you in today?" — rather than a yes/no question.
👂
Active Listening & Reflection
Tap to reveal
Reflect back what the patient says in your own words to show you're following and to check your understanding.
🤝
Normalizing Language
Tap to reveal
Naming that starting an interview can feel uncomfortable lowers a guarded patient's defenses and builds trust faster.
