# Storyboard: Psychiatric Interviewing & Mental Status Examination (Redesigned)

This is the screen-by-screen storyboard for all five microlearning modules
— the document that would hand off directly to development in Articulate
Storyline 360 or Rise 360. Each screen specifies the visual, the on-screen
text, the narration script, the interaction, and where the learner goes
next.

**Format key:** SC = screen, VO = voiceover/narration script.

---

## Storyboard Conventions Used

- **Branching** (Modules 3 and 5) shows students the real consequence of a
  choice before telling them whether it was correct, which is a stronger
  learning moment than a flat right/wrong marker — especially for a
  sensitive skill like risk questioning.
- **Feedback is explanatory, not just evaluative** — every "incorrect"
  response explains why, not just that the answer was wrong.
- **No module exceeds 15 minutes**, keeping every session inside a
  realistic gap between clinical and coursework commitments.
